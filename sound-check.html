Sound check

  1. Turn the ring switch on (not silent) and start music in Spotify or Apple Music.
  2. Come back here and tap 1. Listen: does the music keep going, dip and come back, or stop?
  3. If it stopped, start it again, then try 2, and so on.
  4. Tell Claude the numbers where the music kept going or came back.
